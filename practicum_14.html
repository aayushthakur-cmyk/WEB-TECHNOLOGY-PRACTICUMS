Frequently asked questions

Do I need a prescription for every product?

No. Over-the-counter products can be bought freely. Prescription-only items need a valid prescription from a doctor.

How are PharmaCare products tested?

Raw materials are inspected, each batch is tested for quality, and a final check is done before release.

How should I store my medicines?

Keep them in a cool, dry place away from sunlight and out of reach of children. Always check the expiry date.
